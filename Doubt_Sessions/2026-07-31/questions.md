    # Question 1: Daily Savings Calculator

    ## Problem Statement

    - Accept the amount saved in one day.
- Use a `for` loop to calculate savings for 7 days.
- Print the total.

    ### Sample Input

    ```text
    150
    ```

    ### Sample Output

    ```text
    Total savings after 7 days = 1050
    ```
    ### Explanation

150 × 7 = 1050
